order changes as I learn more, and now and then an idea turns out
not to be worth building. The
[milestones](https://github.com/KiwiCanopy/KiwiDesk/milestones) hold
the full picture, and the
[Discord](https://discord.gg/bYeTrJyZyS) is the place to talk about
any of it.
