# Roadmap

What I'm planning for KiwiDesk next. It's a list, not a promise: the
order changes as I learn more, and now and then an idea turns out
not to be worth building. The
[milestones](https://github.com/KiwiCanopy/KiwiDesk/milestones) hold
the full picture, and the
[Discord](https://discord.gg/bYeTrJyZyS) is the place to talk about
any of it.

I'm building KiwiDesk to be the best window manager for macOS I can
imagine. It has come this far because people tried it and told me
what broke and what they missed. If you have an idea, I'd like to
hear it.
