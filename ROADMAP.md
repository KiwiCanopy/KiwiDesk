# Roadmap

What I'm planning for KiwiDesk next. It's a list, not a promise: the
order changes as I learn more, and now and then an idea turns out
not to be worth building. The
[milestones](https://github.com/KiwiCanopy/KiwiDesk/milestones) hold
the full picture, and the
[Discord](https://discord.gg/bYeTrJyZyS) is the place to talk about
any of it.

I'm building KiwiDesk to be the best window manager for macOS I can
imagine. It has come this far because people tried it and told me
what broke and what they missed. If you have an idea, I'd like to
hear it.

Where it is heading: today KiwiDesk assumes that each screen setup
wants its own configuration. Many people want the opposite: the same
Spaces, splits and settings at home, at the office and on the go,
whatever screens they plug in. The next releases move KiwiDesk
towards that, one step at a time.

## Next on my list

_As of 2026-11-26_

- A look picker: one shortcut opens your looks full screen, step through them with a preview, and apply one, wallpaper included
- Numbered screens: one profile gives every setup with the same number of screens the same Spaces and splits
- Later: one configuration that follows you across setups, whatever the number of screens
